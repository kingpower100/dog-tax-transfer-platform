Moving with a dog in Germany means you have to handle the dog tax (*Hundesteuer*) and registration manually. Because the dog tax is a local municipal tax (*Gemeindesteuer*), **it does not transfer automatically** when you move from one city or municipality to another.

Here is exactly how the process works and where you can find the official information.

### 1\. Deregistration in Your Old Municipality (*Abmeldung*)

Before or shortly after you move, you must officially deregister your dog in your old city.

-   **How to do it:** You can usually do this online, by mail, or in person at your old *Bürgeramt* (Citizens' Registration Office) or *Steueramt* (Tax Office).
    
-   **The Dog Tag:** You will typically be required to return your old physical dog tax tag (*Hundesteuermarke*) to the old municipality.
    
-   **Refunds:** If you have already paid the dog tax for the full year, the old municipality will calculate the tax pro-rata and refund you the difference for the months you no longer live there.
    

### 2\. Registration in Your New Municipality (*Anmeldung*)

Once you arrive in your new city, you must register your dog for the dog tax there.

-   **Deadline:** Most municipalities require you to register your dog within **14 to 30 days** of moving.
    
-   **What you need:** You will usually need your dog’s microchip number, breed information, date of birth, and sometimes proof of liability insurance (*Hundehaftpflichtversicherung*), which is mandatory in several states.
    
-   **The Tax Rate:** Be prepared for a change in costs. Because it is a local tax, the yearly fee varies wildly across Germany (e.g., it might be 90€ in a small town but 120€ in Berlin).
    
-   **New Tag:** You will receive a new *Hundesteuermarke* in the mail, which your dog must wear when outside.
    

### 3\. Other Registrations to Update

In addition to the tax office, do not forget to update your new address in these places:

-   **State Dog Registers (*Hunderegister*):** Some federal states (like Lower Saxony or Berlin) have a mandatory central dog register that is completely separate from the tax office. You must update your address there as well.
    
-   **Pet Registries (TASSO / FINDEFIX):** If your dog is microchipped and registered with a free database like TASSO e.V. (highly recommended in case your dog gets lost), remember to update your address and phone number on their portal.

### Where to find official information and forms (URLs)

Because every municipality handles this slightly differently, there is no single nationwide form. However, the German government provides a central portal to find your specific local requirements:

1.  **The Federal Administration Portal (Verwaltung.bund.de):**
    
    -   **URL:** [https://verwaltung.bund.de/](https://verwaltung.bund.de/)
        
    -   **How to use it:** Go to this site, type "Hundesteuer" into the search bar, and enter the postal code (PLZ) of your **new** municipality. It will route you directly to the correct local office, the exact costs, and the online forms to register your dog.
        
2.  **Your Local City Portal:**
    
    -   The fastest way to find the exact forms is often to bypass the federal portal and search directly for your city's official website.
        
    -   Search online for: `[Name of your new city] Hundesteuer Anmeldung` and `[Name of your old city] Hundesteuer Abmeldung`. Look for official government URLs ending in `.de` (e.g., *berlin.de*, *muenchen.de*).
